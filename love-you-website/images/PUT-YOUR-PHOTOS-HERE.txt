HOW TO USE THIS IMAGES FOLDER
==============================
Developed By Peter Medhat

1. To use your own photos:
   - Place your photos directly inside this "images" folder.
   - For example:
     - images/envelope.jpg
     - images/photo1.jpg
     - images/photo2.jpg
     - images/photo3.jpg
     - images/photo4.jpg

2. Open "config.js" in any text editor (Notepad, VS Code, TextEdit).
3. Ensure the names in config.js match your filenames.
4. You can also paste direct web image URLs (https://...) directly in config.js!
5. Save config.js and double-click index.html to enjoy your website!
